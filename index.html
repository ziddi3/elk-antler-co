Our Story

From the Wild to Happy Dogs

Elk & Antler Co. began with a simple love for dogs and the great outdoors. Our antlers are natural drop-offs and humanely harvested — some are gathered from antlers elk shed naturally each year, while others are cut from the crown and harvested humanely. Always ethically and responsibly sourced.

We hand-gather Grade A antlers, remove any sharp edges, wash them, and meticulously inspect each one before it reaches your pup.

We also offer organic wheat berries — a wholesome whole grain you can sprout into nutritious pet grass or cook as a fiber-rich food topper.
