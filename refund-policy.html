Refund Policy

If an order arrives damaged, incorrect, or materially different from what was ordered, contact us promptly at orders@birchamelkantler.com with your order information and a description of the issue.

Because animal chew products are consumable goods, opened or used products are generally not eligible for return unless they are defective or supplied in error. Unopened eligible items may be reviewed for return or refund on a case-by-case basis.

Approved refunds are issued through the original Methodz/Stripe payment path. Shipping charges may be non-refundable unless the return results from our error.

Do not return an item until return instructions have been provided.
