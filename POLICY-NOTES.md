# Bircham Elk & Antler Co. policy implementation

These storefront policies are operational website policies and should be reviewed against the business's final legal, tax, shipping, and fulfillment practices before launch.

Canonical architecture:
- Methodz collects customer payment through Stripe in CAD.
- Methodz owns the CRM, lead routing, payment orchestration, lifecycle automation, reporting, and settlement path.
- Bircham Elk & Antler Co. is the downstream seller/fulfillment partner and is settled by Methodz after customer payment.
- GoHighLevel is not part of the target architecture.
